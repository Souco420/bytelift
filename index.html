Welcome to ByteLift

ByteLift is a fitness project for people who like clear systems. We treat training the same way programmers treat code: start with a simple version, measure the results and release a better version every few weeks.

Whether you are touching a barbell for the first time or you are chasing a new personal record, you will find a structured plan, a searchable exercise database and practical nutrition advice here – all in one place.

See plans Nutrition tips

Why ByteLift?

Structured

Every plan is split into weeks and sessions, so you always know what to do next.

Measurable

Track sets, reps and weights. Progress you can see is progress you keep.

Healthy

Proper warm-up, technique and recovery are part of every program.

Goal-driven

Strength, muscle or fat loss – choose your goal and we will build the plan.

Choose your version

Every level is a new release. Pick the one that matches your experience.

ByteLift v1.0

Beginner – Hello World

Your first release. Three full-body workouts per week focused on learning the basic movement patterns: squat, hinge, push and pull. Light weights, perfect technique.

  • 3 sessions per week, 45 minutes
  • Machines and dumbbells
  • Duration: 8 weeks
ByteLift v2.0

Advanced – Upper / Lower split

A major update. Four sessions per week split into upper and lower body days. Free weights become the core of the program and progressive overload is tracked every week.

  • 4 sessions per week, 60 minutes
  • Barbell, dumbbells, cables
  • Duration: 12 weeks
ByteLift v3.0

Expert – Push / Pull / Legs

The full release for experienced lifters. Five to six sessions per week, periodization with heavy, medium and light weeks and advanced techniques such as drop sets and supersets.

  • 5–6 sessions per week, 75 minutes
  • Full gym equipment
  • Duration: 16 weeks
