Nutrition

Fuel for your training – simple rules that work.

You can't out-train a bad diet

Training is the input, food is the energy that runs the program. Without enough protein and calories, even the best plan will not bring the results you want.

Good news: you do not need a complicated diet. Follow a few simple rules most of the time, eat mostly real food and adjust the amount based on your goal – building muscle, losing fat or keeping your current weight.

The three macronutrients

Protein

1.6 – 2.2 g / kg

Builds and repairs muscle. Sources: meat, fish, eggs, cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, legumes.

Carbohydrates

3 – 5 g / kg

The main fuel for hard training. Sources: oats, rice, potatoes, whole-grain bread, fruit.

Fats

0.8 – 1 g / kg

Important for hormones and health. Sources: nuts, olive oil, avocado, fatty fish.

5 simple rules

  1. Protein in every meal. Aim for 25–40 g per meal.
  2. Half of the plate is vegetables. Fibre, vitamins, fullness.
  3. Drink water. Around 35 ml per kg of body weight per day.
  4. Plan ahead. Meal prep on Sunday saves the whole week.
  5. 80/20 rule. Eat well 80 % of the time and enjoy the rest.
Supplements are only patches. The real update is your daily food.
